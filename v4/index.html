FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is EduFinder?+
EduFinder is a comparison platform for local IT training in Tampa Bay. You search coding bootcamps, cybersecurity programs, and certification courses, then compare them side by side by price, schedule, format, and reviews.
When does it launch?+
We're launching in the Tampa Bay area soon. Join the pilot at the top of this page and we'll email you the moment we go live — you'll get early access before the public.
Is it free for learners?+
Yes. Searching and comparing providers is completely free for learners. Providers will have optional paid features, but the core comparison experience is always free.
How do you verify providers and reviews?+
We verify provider licensing, credentials, and program information. Reviews come from confirmed enrolled students, with moderation in place to detect fake or manipulated reviews.
Will you cover other types of training?+
Yes — eventually. We're starting with IT training because it's a fast-growing field in Tampa Bay. Over time, we'll expand to language schools, healthcare training, business programs, and more.
