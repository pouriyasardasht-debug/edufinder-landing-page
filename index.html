Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about EduFinder.

What exactly is EduFinder?+
EduFinder is a comparison platform for local IT training in Tampa Bay. You can search coding bootcamps, cybersecurity programs, and certification courses, then compare them side-by-side by price, schedule, format, and reviews.
When will it launch?+
We're launching in the Tampa Bay area soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the moment we go live — you'll get early access before the public.
Is it free for learners?+
Yes. Searching and comparing providers is completely free for learners. Providers will have optional paid features (like premium profiles), but the core comparison experience will always be free.
How do you verify providers and reviews?+
We verify provider licensing, credentials, and program information. Reviews come from confirmed enrolled students, and we have moderation in place to detect fake or manipulated reviews.
Will you cover other types of training?+
Yes — eventually. We're starting with IT training because it's a fast-growing field in Tampa Bay. Over time, we'll expand to language schools, healthcare training, business programs, and more.
