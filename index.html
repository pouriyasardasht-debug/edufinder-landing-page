EduFinder Trust & Transparency Standards

In plain language: how we verify providers, moderate reviews, qualify leads, and rank results — and exactly where paid placement can and cannot influence what you see.

Last updated: Launch Date · Version 1.0

Our Commitment

EduFinder exists because comparing training providers should be easier and more trustworthy than it currently is. That promise only holds if our own comparisons are honest. This page is our public, standing commitment to how we do that — for learners deciding who to trust, and for providers deciding whether our marketplace is fair to compete in.

1

Provider Verification Standards

Every provider listed on EduFinder goes through verification before their profile becomes visible to learners.

Before a listing goes live, we confirm:

  • ✓
    Legal business identity — registered business name, physical or registered address, and a reachable, named point of contact.
  • ✓
    Licensing and authorization, where applicable — for providers offering programs subject to Florida state oversight, we check status against the Florida Commission for Independent Education's public registry.
  • ✓
    Accuracy of listed program information — price, format, duration, and certification claims are checked against the provider's own published materials at the time of listing.
The "Verified Provider" badge is only shown once these checks are complete. A provider without the badge may still appear in search results as "Unverified" while their check is pending, but is never shown ranked above a verified competitor for the same search.
2

Review Standards

Who can review, what we check, and the legal standard we hold ourselves to.

Who can leave a review. Only users who have submitted a documented inquiry to a specific provider through EduFinder, or who confirm enrollment when prompted, can leave a review of that provider.

What we check for. We monitor for patterns commonly associated with manipulated reviews.

Provider right of response. Any provider may post one public response to a review of their listing.

Legal standard we hold ourselves to. Our review practices are built to comply with the FTC's Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 C.F.R. Part 465).
3

Lead Qualification Standards

Not every click is a lead, and not every message is worth the same to a provider.

A lead only counts as qualified, and only becomes billable, if it meets all of the following:

  • 1
    Submitted by a user with a verified, working email address.
  • 2
    Includes a specific indication of interest (a named program, a stated timeline, or a specific question).
  • 3
    Sent by a user session we have reason to believe is genuine.
4

Ranking & Sponsored Content Standards

This is the section we consider most important to state plainly.

How default (organic) results are ordered. Results are ranked by relevance to the learner's search, combined with verification status, average rating, review count, and provider responsiveness.

Payment never affects this ranking. No provider, at any subscription tier, can pay to move up in organic search results.
5

Enforcement & Appeals

What happens when standards aren't met.

Violations can result in a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal for repeated violations.

Any dispute can be raised by emailing trust@edufinder.example. We aim to respond within 5 business days.

6

Updates to This Policy

How this page will evolve as EduFinder grows.

Material changes will be dated and summarized at the top of this page; we will not silently weaken any commitment made here.

Have a question about how we rank or verify?

Our trust team reads every message and responds within 5 business days.
