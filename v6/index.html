The real problem

Finding a course is easy.
Knowing which one to choose isn't.

Learners can find options, but the decision is fragmented. EduFinder is designed around the comparison moment — when someone is close to choosing.

Too many tabs

Price, location, schedule and course details live across different websites. Comparing means jumping between ten browser tabs.

No common language

Programs describe themselves differently, making side-by-side comparison frustrating. No standard format, no clear way to judge.

Trust comes last

Verification and credible reviews should be visible before a learner submits an inquiry — not buried in fine print or discovered too late.
